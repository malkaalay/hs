Artist Story

From Survival To Music

My name is Sam, known as Hicats Sam. I come from Gaza City, and music became the way I could carry what I lived through when words alone were not enough.

I studied Software and Database, but during the war my life changed completely. I lost my home, my laptop, my small studio, and the beginning of the dream I was building. Still, I did not stop writing.

I started writing songs in 2023 during the war, while living through displacement, fear, loss, and survival. I taught myself English because I wanted my story to travel beyond borders and reach people who may never see what life here is really like.

Music became my way forward. I wrote tracks like “Homeless,” “Lost Mom,” and “Bad Story” while trying to survive and hold onto a future.
FEAR is my first released cover track. It took months of work because creating music in Gaza is not simple, but I kept going until it was finally out.
I want to keep recording. Your support helps me stay focused, find a basic way to live, and continue building my voice as an artist.
