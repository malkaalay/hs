Artist Story

From Survival To Music

My name is Sam, I’m 23 years old from Gaza City.

I studied Software and Database at the University College of Applied Science, but my life changed completely at the beginning of the war in 2023.

The day my house was bombed, my entire family was under the rubble ,my mother, my father, my brothers, and my cousins. In that moment, everything I had was gone. I lost my home, my laptop, and the small studio I was building to start my journey in music.

We were forced to leave and move to a school, thinking it was safe, but the bombs never stopped. One day, soldiers surrounded the school and started shooting, forcing us to flee again to the south. During that displacement, I saw things I will never forget , bodies on the ground and dogs eating them.

That moment will stay with me forever.

We ended up living in tents. In the middle of fear and loss, I started writing music. I wrote tracks like 'Homeless , Lost Mom , Bad Story ' , I kept listening to rap and teaching myself English because I wanted my story to reach beyond Gaza.

For years, I wasn’t living. I was just trying to survive each day while holding on to the idea that music could still be my future.

When I returned, there was no home left. My entire neighborhood was destroyed, and even now I live in a tent with my family.

Life here is extremely hard, but I never stopped.

Along the way, I met people who believed in me. Qasam helped me record. Raji is building a rock band in Gaza. Billy from the UK helped me improve my flow. Garry from Ireland pushed me to grow. They became real support in a place where support is rare.

After everything, I released my first track, 'FEAR' by NF. It took me five months, not because I wanted it to, but because creating music in Gaza is not easy. Still, I did everything I could to bring it to life.

My Cover Track Fear by NF Is Out Now, and this is just the beginning.

If you feel something while listening, then you already understand. Your support doesn’t just help my music; it helps me keep going, survive, and continue telling this story to the world.
